# Agent instructions

## Foundational Training Architecture

Before modifying any exercise prescription, strength-duration modeling, coaching, or training analysis functionality, read [docs/training-philosophy.md](docs/training-philosophy.md).

Its principles are authoritative design requirements.

Do not introduce coaching logic that contradicts the training philosophy, overrides curve-driven prescriptions, interprets normal maximal-effort outcomes as training problems, or generates recommendations without sufficient supporting evidence.

Preserve the separation between prescription, behavioral coaching, and analysis/research.

If a proposed implementation conflicts with these principles, identify the conflict before proceeding.
